# LÉMAQQUE

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-specified React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, shadcn-style Radix components, React Router, TanStack Query, React Hook Form, Zod, Recharts, Three.js, R3F v9, drei, GSAP, Supabase and Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

People tracking gym or home training, nutrition and body progress, including Malaysian food contexts. Programs must adapt for beginners.

## Product Purpose

Train. Fuel. Evolve. A persistent daily fitness companion with optional immersive 3D guidance.

## Brand Commitments

LÉMAQQUE, monogram LMQ. Obsidian, charcoal, active lime, champagne and ivory. Luxury athletic, dark first, cinematic but practical.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The supplied master brief is authoritative. Functional MVP precedes advanced assets and optional AI. Never fabricate statistics or nutrition values. Never represent unvalidated animations as authoritative technique. Do not claim athlete endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand

No user activity, licensed anatomy assets, validated animation clips, food composition database, or Supabase credentials supplied at implementation start.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard alternatives, reduced motion, mobile-first forms, and full tracking without WebGL. Server-enforced isolation for cloud accounts.
